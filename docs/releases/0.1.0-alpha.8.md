# Modern Vyapari v0.1.0-alpha.8

This alpha makes the plan journey hold up in real use. A plan was taken from research to a
reviewed trade in the running app on the live price stream, then pushed off the happy path,
and every seam that walk hit is fixed. All trading is still simulated.

## Plans

- A plan whose entry is "at market" now fills at the close of the bar that triggered it.
  Before, it could fail on its first trigger and keep showing *Armed*.
- Arming checks that each level is complete, so a plan can't be armed without a stop.
- Invalidation or expiry now closes an open position. A plan that fired and then expires
  ends as closed.
- Bars are judged at their close, so expiry is no longer a bar late. A stop that the price
  gaps through fills at the bar's open.
- After a restart or an outage, missed bars are replayed against open positions.
- The kill switch stays on across restarts. Home leads with it and names paused sessions.
- Arming warns when a plan's budget is more than the simulated cash, and every plan's
  branch budgets must total 100% or less.
- Arm, save, discard, end and revise act once per click. The arm confirmation names the mode
  ("Simulated — a trigger opens a simulated position. No real order is placed.").
- The plan card lists all of its terms. A refused entry reads *Fired · no entry*.

## Reviews and the agent

- Trade and plan reviews are written again. They had been coming back empty.
- A plan drafted at a 1-minute interval keeps that interval.
- The agent reports expiry as a time and minutes left, and no longer reports a refused entry
  as an open position.

## Screens

- Home, session overview, Portfolio and Orders update as soon as data changes.
- In simulated mode, Portfolio no longer shows an exchange balance.
- Plan and engine events stay in the notification history.
- The status badges read *Prices · Binance live* and *Orders simulated*.

## App

- Windows Hello shows one unlock prompt at a time, and failed or cancelled unlocks appear in
  the activity feed.
- A quit that you cancel leaves alerts and plans running.
- An installed build won't start with a remote-debugging switch.
- Dependency updates, including Electron 45.2.2.

## Known limits

- Plans are monitored only while the app is running.
- Live order placement isn't available. All fills are simulated estimates.
- Installers are unsigned. Windows is tested; macOS and Linux builds are not.
